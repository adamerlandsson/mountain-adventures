August 3, 2026

Summit of the Misty Peak

Today was unforgettable. We woke up at 4 AM and started our hike while the stars were still visible above. The climb was challenging, especially the last 500 meters with thin air and steep switchbacks. But when we reached the summit just as the sun was rising, all the effort melted away. The view was breathtaking — endless ridges stretching out in every direction, and the valley below completely shrouded in mist.

We spent an hour at the top eating energy bars and soaking in the moment. Worth every drop of sweat.

July 28, 2026

Forest Trail Discovery

Found a hidden trail today while exploring the backcountry near Eagle Lake. It winds through old-growth forest with towering pines and the occasional clearings that reveal stunning lake views. We saw a family of deer grazing near the water and heard the distant call of an eagle.

This trail doesn't seem to get much traffic — fallen logs across the path and minimal signage — but it's absolutely worth the extra scrambling. Definitely coming back here next month.

July 15, 2026

Alpine Lake Camping

Three-day backpacking trip to the alpine lake was exactly what I needed. The water is impossibly clear — you can see straight to the bottom even in the deepest spots. We set up camp on a small ridge overlooking the lake and spent the evening watching the light change as the sun moved behind the western peaks.

The sunrise the next morning was even better. Woke up to find mist rising off the water and a golden glow lighting up the granite cliffs. These moments remind me why I love the mountains so much.
